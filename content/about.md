title: "A Century of Fullerton Heritage."
story_1: |
  Troop 93 is a high adventure troop in Fullerton, where outdoor challenges help Scouts develop confidence, teamwork, and leadership. We believe in the core values of Scouting and make vigorous use of the BSA Patrol Method, which emphasizes boy leadership and personal responsibility.

  Our Troop program is planned and executed by the Scouts themselves, based on their interests and the needs of the Troop. The advancement program is supported by Troop activities, but the pace of an individual Scout’s advancement is dependent, to a great degree, on his own initiative.

  There is room for every Scout, whatever their interests or experience. Camping, community service, STEM activities, merit badges, and leadership offer many ways to participate and contribute. Our outdoor program ranges from first-year experiences to high adventure for older, more experienced Scouts. Each Scout can discover what excites them and grow at their own pace.
story_2: |
  Leadership skills are developed through the use of the Patrol Method and the Patrol Leaders Council (monthly planning meeting incorporating the patrol leaders with adult guidance).

  Self-discipline and self-sufficiency of the young men is developed through monthly camping opportunities. Individual accomplishment is met through advancement within the Boy Scout program.

  Troop 93 is guided using the “two deep” leadership method by registered and trained adult leaders at all times during each meeting and function. All adult leaders are required to have current Youth Protection certification and have completed all leader specific training as required for their position in the Troop.

  New leaders and committee members are always welcome. Parental involvement is a very important ingredient to maintaining our strong Troop.
